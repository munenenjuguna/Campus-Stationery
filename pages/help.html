Ordering

Open Shop, click Add to Cart on the items you want, then open your cart and click Proceed to Checkout. You need to be logged in to check out. Choose pickup or delivery, choose how to pay and click Place Order.

The cart never lets you order more than we have in stock. If an item shows Only 3 left, the most you can order is 3.

Payment

After you place the order, an M-Pesa request appears on the phone number you entered. Enter your M-Pesa PIN to pay. The page confirms the payment automatically and shows the M-Pesa receipt number.

Your order is still saved. Open My Orders and click Pay with M-Pesa to send the request again.

Your account

Click Forgot password? on the login page and enter your email. We send you a link that works for 30 minutes.

An extra lock on your account. When it is on, every login asks for your password and then a 6 digit code we email to you, so nobody can get in with your password alone. Switch it on or off in My Account. The code works once and expires after 5 minutes.

After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 5 minutes to keep it safe. Wait, then try again or reset your password.

Open My Account from the menu under your name.
